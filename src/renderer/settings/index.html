        🎨 How to make your character (AI image-prompt recipe)

        Generate two images of the same character on a plain pale-blue background, full body with empty space around them. Make the idle pose first, then the action pose in the same chat so the tool matches the character.

        1. Idle pose - standing, holding a water bottle.
        2. Action pose - drinking from the bottle.

        3 rules: plain solid background (Nudzie cuts it out) · full body centered with empty space · same character in both images.

        Upload both idle and action poses to use this character.

        Your images are processed on your device and never uploaded anywhere.
