Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

The short version. Nudzie runs entirely on your device. It has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. Your calendar events are read on your computer and never sent to us or anyone else. We do not collect, see, store, sell, or share your personal data - because there is nowhere on our side for it to go.

Who this covers

This policy explains how the Nudzie desktop application ("Nudzie", "the app", "we", "us") handles information. It applies to the app you install and run on macOS or Windows. Nudzie is a local-first application: there is no Nudzie account, no Nudzie cloud, and no Nudzie backend server.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Nudzie does not send your data to us. Specifically:

  • No account. You do not create a Nudzie account or give us an email, name, or password.
  • No analytics or telemetry. Nudzie does not track usage, crashes, clicks, or behavior, and includes no third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No advertising. Nudzie contains no ads and no advertising identifiers.

Calendar data

If you connect a calendar (Google Calendar, Apple iCloud, or an iCal/.ics link), Nudzie reads your upcoming events directly from that provider to your device so it can show you a reminder before a meeting. This data:

  • is accessed read-only - Nudzie never creates, edits, or deletes events;
  • is kept only in memory on your device while the app is running, used solely to display the next reminders;
  • is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and is not written to disk as event content.

Google user data & Limited Use

When you connect Google Calendar, Nudzie requests the calendar.events.readonly scope to read your upcoming events on your device. Nudzie's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In plain terms: information from Google is used only to show your reminders inside Nudzie, on your device. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never transferred to others except as needed to provide this feature, and is never read by humans.

The read-only access token that authorizes this is stored only on your device (see "Where things are stored"). You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Calendars, which removes the stored token, or revoke access from your Google Account permissions.

Where things are stored

Everything Nudzie saves is stored locally in your operating system's per-user application-data folder. Nothing is uploaded. This includes:

  • Sign-in credentials you choose to keep - a Google refresh token, an Apple app-specific password, or iCal feed URLs - are stored only if you enable "Stay signed in to calendars", and are encrypted at rest using your operating system's secure storage (Keychain on macOS, DPAPI/Credential store on Windows). Turn the setting off and nothing is stored.
  • Your preferences (reminder settings, active hours, chosen character, theme, etc.).
  • Personalization you add, such as a custom character image or a custom sound file - processed and kept on your device only.

Third-party services

When you connect a calendar, your device communicates directly with that provider (Google, Apple, or the host of an iCal link). Your use of those services is governed by their own privacy policies. Nudzie is not a party to that data exchange beyond your own device requesting your own data.

Children

Nudzie is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them (we do not collect information from anyone).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the new version at this URL.
