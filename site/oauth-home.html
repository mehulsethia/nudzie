Nudzie

Nudzie is a desktop reminder app for macOS and Windows. A small pixel buddy lives in the corner of your screen and shows friendly reminder nudges for meetings, breaks, water, habits, medicines, birthdays, and anything else you set.

What Nudzie does

  • Shows manual, one-off, and repeating reminders on your desktop.
  • Lets you accept, complete, or snooze reminders.
  • Connects calendar sources so Nudzie can remind you before upcoming meetings.

Why Nudzie asks for Google Calendar access

Nudzie uses Google Calendar read-only access to find upcoming calendar events and show meeting reminders on your device. Nudzie does not create, edit, or delete Google Calendar events.

Calendar data is processed on your device for reminder functionality. Nudzie does not upload Google Calendar event data to a Nudzie server, sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with humans.

Google OAuth app identity

The Google OAuth consent screen app name is Nudzie. This page, the product homepage, privacy policy, and terms are all hosted on the verified Nudzie domain.
