Zero data. No servers.

Nudzie has no backend and no database. Your calendar is read directly on your device, kept only in memory, and never written to disk or sent anywhere. No telemetry, no analytics. The only thing it can store is your encrypted sign-in token - and only if you ask it to.

questions & answers

Good to know

Which calendars work?

Google Calendar, Apple iCloud (via CalDAV), and any public iCal / .ics link. It's read-only - Nudzie never edits or deletes your events.

Does it work over fullscreen apps?

Yes. The buddy floats above everything, including fullscreen video calls, and never blocks your clicks - it stays out of the way until it's time to nudge.

What's free, and what's Pro?

Free gives you the Buddy character, one calendar or interval, and your own message and sound. Pro is a one-time $6.99 and unlocks more characters and sounds, custom themes, bring-your-own pixel art, and combining unlimited calendars.

What about my privacy?

Everything runs on your device. No servers, no telemetry. Calendar data stays in memory; only an encrypted sign-in token can be saved, and only with your permission.

Which platforms?

macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel) and Windows (x64 & ARM). Same buddy, same features, on whichever desktop you use. Linux can follow.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit from the menu bar and drag the app to the Trash. Nothing is left behind on a server - because there is no server.
